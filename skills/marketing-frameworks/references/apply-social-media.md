# Playbook: social media

**When to use this file:** social media strategy, content pillars, calendars, hooks and captions, paid social, community management, short-form video, or an audit of an existing account. Platform-neutral: adapt the tactics to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Facebook or others.

Why this order: social is where Reach happens and where most attention is wasted. Knowing who you are talking to (STP) and what behavior you want (TPB) keeps posts from becoming noise. Brand goals decide the content mix; the touchpoint idea and the Eisenhower matrix keep the calendar realistic.

## Workflow

1. **Set the goal and the behavior.** Not "grow followers" alone: which action should social cause (visit, enquire, book, subscribe, share)? Take the baseline from the account's own analytics ([TPB](theory-of-planned-behavior.md), [RACE](race-framework.md)).
2. **STP for the account.** Pick the audience segment and write the positioning statement. The bio is that statement shortened ([STP](stp-model.md)).
3. **Pick the brand pyramid stage.** New or unknown account: salience (be recognisable, repeat distinctive assets). Known but unproved: performance and judgments (demos, reviews, results). Already trusted: imagery, feelings and community ([brand pyramid](brand-resonance-pyramid.md)).
4. **Choose 3-5 content pillars** from that stage and the audience's needs. Each pillar maps to a RACE stage: Reach (shareable, discovery), Act (useful, saveable), Convert (offer, proof), Engage (community, customers).
5. **Map the touchpoints.** Social is one touch in a longer path. Plan a sequence across posts, stories, email and the website, with frequency caps on paid and varied creative. Do not rely on a magic number of views ([rule of 7](rule-of-7.md)).
6. **Write hooks and captions with TPB and Zeigarnik.** Hook = curiosity gap or clear benefit. Open loops ("part 2 on Thursday", "the third mistake is the costly one") need a payoff, or trust drops. Caption body addresses the weak TPB factor, and the call to action makes the next step easy ([Zeigarnik](zeigarnik-effect.md)).
7. **Build the calendar.** Use the template below. Balance pillars and RACE stages across weeks.
8. **Prioritise with Eisenhower.** Evergreen and series content is Schedule, reactive trend posts are Do or Drop, and vanity tasks are dropped ([Eisenhower](eisenhower-matrix.md)).
9. **Paid social (if used).** Segment audiences by need, cap frequency, rotate creative, retarget warm audiences. Test offers; if showing tiers, treat any decoy as an A/B hypothesis ([decoy](decoy-effect.md)).
10. **Community and creators.** Reply fast, feature customers (norms). For creators and staff, brief with specific high but believable expectations plus support ([Pygmalion](pygmalion-effect.md)).
11. **Review monthly.** Compare to baseline, double down on the pillar and format that moved the target behavior, cut the rest.

Frameworks that fit weakly: **decoy** only matters if posts or ads show pricing options. **4 Ps** is best used once for the account (what Product, Price, Place, Promotion social supports), not per post ([4 Ps](4ps-marketing-mix.md)).

## Framework to tactics to measures

| Framework | Tactics on social | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| STP | One audience, positioning-led bio, content speaks to their need | Follower and engager profile vs target segment |
| TPB | Hook for attitude, testimonials and UGC for norms, link-in-bio and simple steps for control | Link clicks, enquiries, bookings, DM-to-order rate |
| RACE | Pillars mapped to stages | Reach: reach and impressions; Act: saves, shares, profile visits; Convert: orders; Engage: repeat buyers, replies |
| Brand pyramid | Pillar mix chosen by weakest stage; distinctive assets repeated | Branded search, mentions, review count, community activity |
| Rule of 7 | Sequenced posts across formats, retargeting with caps, creative rotation | Frequency, CTR trend, cost per result trend |
| Zeigarnik | Series and open loops, "finish your booking" prompts, carousels with a payoff | Series return rate, completion or watch-through, retarget conversions |
| Eisenhower | Calendar triage, batch the Schedule items | Posts shipped on plan, share of effort on top pillars |
| Decoy | Tiered offer creative tested against plain version | Target-option share |
| Pygmalion | Creator and staff briefs, feedback loops | Creator output quality, revisions needed |

## Worked example: yoga studio, 4-week plan

Business: "Still Point", a neighbourhood yoga studio with 3 teachers. Goal: more first-class bookings from locals who have never tried yoga. Baseline: bookings per week from the booking tool and profile visits from account analytics, to be recorded first.

- **STP:** target: adults new to yoga, put off by feeling out of place. Position: "For beginners, Still Point is the neighbourhood studio where your first class is paced for you, with small groups and a teacher who walks you through each pose."
- **TPB:** attitude is fine; norms (everyone else is experienced) and control (don't know what to wear or do) are weak.
- **Pyramid:** modest salience, little proof, so lead with performance and judgments: real beginner classes and student reviews.
- **Pillars:** (1) Beginner basics (Act), (2) Student stories (Convert, norms), (3) Studio life and teachers (Engage, imagery), (4) Quick home sequences (Reach, shareable).
- **Week 1:** carousel "What to expect at your first class" (hook: the three questions every beginner is afraid to ask); a Reel of a teacher demonstrating a pose; story polls. CTA: book a first class.
- **Week 2:** student story post with consent; short video "Part 1 of 3: poses I wish I knew at the start", with part 2 and 3 scheduled (open loop with a payoff); Friday reminder story.
- **Week 3:** studio tour reel; teacher intro; buddy-pass offer to bring a friend (norms and control).
- **Week 4:** recap of the series, FAQ highlight pinned, review ask to attendees, retarget video viewers with the booking link, capped frequency.
- **Eisenhower:** Do: pinned FAQ, booking link. Schedule: series, reviews. Drop: chasing every trend audio.
- **Pygmalion:** the front-desk helper who films sessions gets a short brief, an example reel and weekly feedback.
- **Review:** first-class bookings and profile visits vs baseline, saves and shares per pillar, which hook style drove booking clicks.

## Common mistakes

- Chasing followers instead of the behavior that earns revenue.
- Posting in all formats and platforms with no audience or pillars.
- Open loops that never pay off.
- Quoting a posting frequency or engagement rate as a universal benchmark instead of comparing to your own history.
- Treating the rule of 7 as a posting quota.
- Heavy brand-community talk before anyone can recall or trust the brand.
- Copying a competitor's content without checking it fits your position.
- Ignoring replies and comments, which is where norms and trust are built.
- Putting everything in "urgent" trend reactions and dropping the compounding series.
