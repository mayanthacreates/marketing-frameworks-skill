# RACE Framework (Plan, Reach, Act, Convert, Engage)

**What it is:** A digital marketing planning framework created by Dave Chaffey at Smart Insights in 2010. It splits the customer lifecycle into stages so every activity and KPI has a home. Often written as **PRACE** because Plan comes first.

## The stages

| Stage | Goal | Typical activities | Example KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | Set strategy before tactics | Goals, audience research, budget, measurement setup | Targets agreed, tracking live |
| **Reach** | Get found by the right people | SEO, paid media, social, PR, partnerships | Unique visitors, impressions, share of search |
| **Act** | Get a first interaction | Content, lead magnets, landing pages, product pages | Engagement rate, time on page, leads |
| **Convert** | Turn interest into a sale | CRO, retargeting, checkout fixes, sales follow-up | Conversion rate, revenue, CPA |
| **Engage** | Keep and grow customers | Email, loyalty, onboarding, community, reviews | Repeat purchase rate, retention, NPS, LTV |

## How to apply it

1. Write one measurable goal for the whole plan (Plan).
2. For each stage, list what is happening now, what is missing and one KPI.
3. Find the weakest stage. That is usually where the money is. Lots of traffic with few leads means fix Act, not Reach.
4. Assign owners and a review cadence per stage.

## Worked example

A local bakery with a good Instagram following but flat online orders: Reach is fine (12k followers), Act is weak (no link to ordering in bio, no menu highlights), Convert is weak (ordering is by DM). Fix Act and Convert first: a bio link to an order page, pinned menu posts and a simple checkout. Engage then adds a reorder reminder by WhatsApp or email.

## Common mistakes

- Pouring budget into Reach when the leak is in Convert.
- Treating Engage as "post on social" instead of retention of *existing customers*.
- KPIs that are vanity metrics (likes) rather than stage outcomes.

## Name clash: RACE for AI prompts

There is an unrelated prompt-engineering acronym also called RACE: **Role, Action, Context, Expectation** (tell the AI who to be, what to do, the background, and what the output should look like). If the user means prompting, use that. If they mean marketing planning, use Chaffey's RACE above. Ask when unclear.

## Evidence

Practitioner planning model. Useful structure, widely used, not an empirically tested theory.

Source: Smart Insights, "Introducing the RACE Growth System" (smartinsights.com).
