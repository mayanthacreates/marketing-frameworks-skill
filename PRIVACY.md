# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

**Marketing Frameworks** (this skill and plugin) collects no data.

- It is made only of Markdown instruction files. It contains no code, no scripts, no MCP servers, no hooks and no tracking.
- It does not read, store or send personal data, and it does not contact any server operated by the author or any third party.
- Anything you type, and any files or numbers you share while using it, are handled solely by the AI tool you run it in (for example Claude, Codex or Gemini CLI), under that tool's own privacy policy. The author never receives it.
- The skill may suggest that your agent look up public documentation (for example an ad platform's help pages). Those lookups are made by your AI tool, not by this plugin.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
